Notes

This work appears in the online PDF catalogue for Ron English: Brand Royalty, where it is listed as Delusionville Paint Jam, 2021, oil on canvas, 72 × 90 inches.

The work appears at 0:18 in the trailer for the documentary Living in Delusionville.

This composition was later reproduced by the artist as a limited-edition print in the Vinyl Chord: A Revolutionary Record Store series.

Additional documentary material from Andrew B. D. Porter’s Living in Delusionville page is retained below as supporting production/context documentation.
